# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

CloudPulse is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone who participates in the project.

We welcome contributors regardless of experience level, background, identity, education, or technical expertise.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and constructively
- welcome questions from new contributors
- give useful and considerate feedback
- respect different viewpoints and experiences
- focus technical discussions on ideas rather than individuals
- accept constructive criticism gracefully
- help maintain a collaborative open-source community

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment or discrimination
- personal attacks or insults
- intimidation or threats
- deliberately disruptive behavior
- publishing another persons private information without permission
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention
- repeated hostile or disrespectful communication

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to CloudPulse project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and other project-related communication.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project participation.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer privately rather than escalating the situation publicly.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. Information about a report should be shared only when necessary to investigate and resolve the situation.

## Our Goal

CloudPulse should be a project where people can learn, contribute, disagree constructively, and improve the software together.
